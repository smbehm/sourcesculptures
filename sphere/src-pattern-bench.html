Pattern is an analytic signed field on the surface direction — seamless, no UV wrap. Eight families are pole-free; parastichy slots and loxodromes are two-pole, because banding a lattice into spirals makes a direction field and the hairy-ball theorem forces total index +2. Open area and cut path are measured on the GPU from the actual field — validated to 0.5% against closed form — not estimated per pattern.
