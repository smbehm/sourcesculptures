# Eva Cavalli birthday film — reference archive

Collected 6 October 2026. 32 distinct, visually reviewed image files, organized by character and era where documented.

Open `gallery.html` to browse all saved pictures and their sources. `manifest.csv` is the simple file inventory; `manifest.json` contains the same metadata. `research_leads.csv` lists 16 useful leads, exclusions and access limitations. Empty character/era folders contain a research status note.

## Coverage and gaps

Eva: 2007 launch, 2014 runway/yacht, 2015 birthday, 2016 and 2018 events, an undated portrait, and the 2024 photograph with Naomi stored in Naomi's shared-photo folder. Added a 1977 finalist group, pageant-era dress fitting (exact year unverified), and captioned 1987 photo. Childhood and 1990s remain gaps. Verified pageant scans were rejected for visible watermarks.

Roberto: early undated portrait, vineyard image with Tommaso, and joint photographs with Eva. Rachele: editorial portrait. Daniele: 2020 interview portrait. Robert: 2015 party and 2019 design interview. Tommaso: estate, horse and wine photographs. Cristiana: official Krimrose founder portrait, undated, and 2022 intoscana portrait. Marta: magazine and personal archive photographs from the 1970s/1980s and undated images. Naomi: 2024 with Eva.

Cindy with Eva, Frieda Koenig, Anton Düringer and grandchildren have no retained qualifying files. See their folder notes. Shared photos are stored once; the manifest's subjects field cross-references everyone relevant. No identities were assigned solely from face similarity.

## Resolution and selection

These are the best accessible versions found in the checked sources, not a claim that no larger photograph exists elsewhere. Publisher masters/original upload paths were preferred to thumbnails. Naomi with Eva is 4118x2745; Eva's tallest portrait is 2832x4256. Some older Marta images and Robert's 2019 portrait are moderate-resolution references, explicitly marked in the manifest. ELLE's advertised CDN rendition is not a verified camera original.

No upscaling, retouching, watermark removal or image cropping was performed. Saved image bytes and embedded metadata are preserved. Exact-file duplicates were checked with SHA-256; visible duplicate crops, unrelated pictures, tiny files and watermarked scans were excluded. Publication years, issue dates and actual capture dates are distinguished in the manifest. Undated photographs remain undated.

## Sources and reuse

Sources include Vogue, British Vogue, Vogue Italia, Vanity Fair Italia, ELLE, WOMAN, intoscana, Firenze Made in Tuscany, Fucking Young!, Krimrose, Amica, Kitzbueheler Anzeiger, History of Beauty, and Tenuta degli Dei. Photographers are credited where established. Public availability is not a reuse licence: this is a reference collection, and no publication or film-use permissions were purchased. Some IMAGO files retain territorial publication restrictions, flagged in the manifest.

For the opening family scenes, the best next source is the family's own childhood, wedding and young-family archive. For clean pageant originals and Cindy with Eva, use the agency asset links in research_leads.csv.
