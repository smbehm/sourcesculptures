# Film location references

Saved original public uploads, with dimensions, credit, licence metadata and proposed shot use in the manifest. Modern location photos establish architecture, geography and atmosphere, not period accuracy. The 1975 Dornbirn image is the archival exception. No AI upscaling or cropping was applied.

Covered: Dornbirn, Lake Constance, Santo Domingo theatre auditorium, Florence city, Boboli garden inspiration, Costa Smeralda beach inspiration and Tenuta degli Dei vineyard/cellar context.

Still required: Eva’s actual Austrian family home; private Florence house/interiors/garden; 1977 backstage; exact Sardinian family terrace and dinner layout; period atelier/textile workshop; actual Milan runway/backstage and Robert/Rachele/Daniele work spaces; Eva’s current preferred location; family homes; final dream-table set design. These should be supplied by the family or deliberately designed as reconstructions. Boboli is not the family garden and Capriccioli is not a verified Cavalli beach.

Several Wikimedia originals returned rate-limit responses; unacquired files were not represented as saved. Photographer and licence details are preserved where supplied. Tourism images are reference copies without an acquired film-use licence. Public-domain/Creative Commons entries require their recorded conditions to be checked for the intended use.
